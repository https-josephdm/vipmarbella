Your Life. Your Property. Our Service.

VIP Service Marbella is led by its owner, Michel Stoppe, born Von Brück. We look after homes, yachts and vehicles on the Costa del Sol. Electrical, gas, water, pool and spa, heating and air conditioning work, full property management and care for your fleet are handled by one team that keeps every detail in order, with the discretion and reliability you expect.

Beyond the technical side, our concierge desk arranges yacht charters, helicopter rentals, special transport and personal butler service on request, and we offer professional security tailored to you and your property. We work in English and German and shape every service around your individual wishes. You tell us what you need. We take care of making it happen.
